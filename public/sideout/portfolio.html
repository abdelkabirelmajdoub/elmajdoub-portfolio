Sideout is a volleyball match finder concept, designed around the simple joy of getting people onto the same court.

Explore the project ↗

Less scrolling.
More volleyball.

A court-blue identity, a hand-built illustration, and a reservation flow that works entirely in your browser.

The idea

Make casual matches easy to browse. Beach and indoor filters let players find their setting, while a level selector narrows the choices.

The build

Semantic HTML, responsive CSS, and JavaScript. Reservations persist with localStorage, with an in-memory fallback when storage is unavailable.

The design

White court lines and a yellow volleyball provide the visual language. The large court illustration carries the personality, leaving match information clear and calm.

The details

Keyboard focus, live reservation feedback, reduced-motion support, mobile layouts, and explicit demo labels. All matches are illustrative; there are no real bookings.
